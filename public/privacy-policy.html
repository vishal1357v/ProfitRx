Privacy Policy for ProfitRx

Effective Date: July 4, 2026

Summary: ProfitRx ("Greek God SaaS") provides profit intelligence, COD management, and GST tax reporting for Shopify merchants. We prioritize data privacy and comply with the Indian Information Technology Act 2000 and GDPR guidelines.

1. Information We Collect

  • Shopify Store Data: Shop domain, currency, timezone, and order summaries (total price, fulfillment status, shipping pincode, line item IDs).
  • COD & Customer Data: Masked phone numbers, pincodes, and order delivery status used solely for RTO risk detection and OTP verification.
  • Merchant Contact Data: Account owner email address and GSTIN for tax reporting.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use collected store data exclusively to:

  • Calculate net profit margins after deducting COGS, gateway fees, and logistics freight overages.
  • Provide pincode-level RTO risk heatmaps and block high-risk COD orders.
  • Generate downloadable GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B tax summary reports.

3. Data Storage & Protection

All data is stored in encrypted PostgreSQL database clusters hosted on Neon with SSL/TLS encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest. We do not sell or monetize merchant data.

4. Data Retention & Mandatory Deletion Webhooks

Order transaction logs are retained for 90 days. Aggregated financial statistics are retained for store reporting. Upon app uninstallation, we process Shopify's mandatory data deletion webhooks (customers/redact, shop/redact) within 48 hours.

5. Contact Us

For data privacy requests or support queries, contact us at: support@profitrx.app.
